## Recent decisions and rationale

- **VolumeWard is the reference methodology.** The owner and Chat establish requirements, architecture, and acceptance criteria together. Once authorized, a single engineering contract goes to the implementer and independent reviewer. It specifies scope, permissions, permitted changes, evidence requirements, acceptance criteria, and stop conditions.
- **Bounded implementation and review.** Claude implements and produces evidence. Work independently reviews the exact candidate and returns findings. Claude remediates in-scope findings and Work verifies the revised candidate. The loop ends at the agreed limit, on a blocker or scope change, or when the result is ready for owner review. Escalate unresolved decisions and deliver the candidate, evidence, and residual findings to the owner for acceptance.
- **Cloud-backed execution.** The proposed local orchestration layer coordinates subscription-backed cloud frontier models through supported accounts and interfaces. Architecture and requirements deliberation stay with the owner and Chat. Provider and model portability remain design goals. Local model execution may serve suitable bounded tasks.

### Shared visual identity

The approved project family establishes a common LaboratoryZero visual language comprising:

- A shared circular reticle with radial markings.
- Distinct project-specific geometric emblems.
- Individual signature accent colours.
- Bold uppercase project wordmarks.
- Concise mission descriptors.
- Consistent presentation and visual proportions.

The identity system is intended to accommodate future projects without requiring redesign of existing project identities.

The current seven projects represent the existing portfolio, not a fixed organizational limit.

### Proposed branding governance architecture

Preserve the following architecture as a proposed implementation direction, distinct from the approved visual designs.

#### Shared project index

`LaboratoryZero/project-index` would retain authority over:

- LaboratoryZero visual identity standards.
- Master asset catalogue.
- Shared design specifications.
- Cross-project branding conventions.

#### OVERWATCH

OVERWATCH would eventually coordinate:

- Branding consistency.
- Standards compliance.
- New project identity onboarding.
- Cross-project asset lifecycle governance.
- Identification of unauthorized visual drift.

OVERWATCH coordinates governance without displacing human approval authority or individual project ownership.

#### Individual project repositories

Each project would retain its own approved branding assets and repository presentation.

Existing project-specific creative canon remains unaffected, including FOX character identities and universe-specific visual references.

The proposed common structure is:

### OVERWATCH strategic rationale

OVERWATCH is not an unrelated expansion of the LaboratoryZero portfolio.

Its establishment consolidates governance and operational practices already emerging across existing projects:

- PAOF: separation of orchestration, execution and monitoring.
- ECHO: persistence, memory and continuity.
- VolumeWard: engineering governance, independent review and evidence-backed acceptance.
- Project Index: shared authority and cross-project consistency.
- FOX: identity preservation, canon governance and creative authority.

Recent VolumeWard engineering activities provide practical evidence of the increasing coordination overhead associated with multi-agent engineering, independent review, repository operations and continuity management.

OVERWATCH is intended to reduce that overhead through a coherent governance and orchestration architecture.

VolumeWard should continue to serve as a practical source of requirements and operational evidence without becoming dependent upon an unfinished OVERWATCH implementation.

The existing decision to defer OVERWATCH development remains unchanged.

**OVERWATCH is infrastructure for managing project complexity, not justification for uncontrolled portfolio expansion.**
