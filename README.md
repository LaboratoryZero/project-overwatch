# project-overwatch
Orchestration, Verification and Engineering Regulation through Workflow Assurance, Traceability, Continuity and Harmonization

Project OVERWATCH — Founding concept
A model-agnostic governance, collaboration and orchestration framework for the LaboratoryZero project ecosystem.

Its purpose is to develop the shared methodologies, protocols and mechanisms through which human and artificial intelligence collaborate across technical, operational, philosophical and creative disciplines, while preserving project autonomy, traceability, continuity and human authority.

Founding responsibilities
Domain	Responsibility
Governance	Develop shared policies, authorization boundaries and decision protocols.
Orchestration	Coordinate human and multi-agent collaboration.
Engineering assurance	Establish evidence-backed implementation and independent verification methodologies.
Exploratory control	Maintain distinctions between exploration, proposals, approval and canon.
Continuity	Define cross-project context and handoff requirements in collaboration with ECHO.
Interoperability	Maintain model, provider and tooling independence.
Traceability	Preserve provenance, accountability and reproducibility.
Harmonization	Maintain consistency across projects without erasing their individual characteristics.

The boundaries matter
I would establish these distinctions from the outset:
- OVERWATCH develops the governance methodology.
- The shared index publishes authoritative cross-project governance records.
- PAOF provides operational automation capabilities.
- ECHO retains responsibility for identity, memory and continuity.
- Individual projects retain their respective domain authorities.
- You retain ultimate authorization.
OVERWATCH should never become an autonomous authority over the ecosystem it is intended to serve.

That would be a fundamental architectural error.
